Parks, Trails, and Open Space

Orem's parks and trails are minutes from every neighborhood, from splash pads downtown to mountain roads up Provo Canyon.

  • Splash pads and fountains for summer afternoons
  • Scenic drives into the Wasatch Mountains
  • Open meadows and picnic areas in city parks

Orem City Days

Every summer, Orem City Days brings neighbors together for a parade, live music, food trucks, and fireworks over the valley. Volunteers and local vendors are always welcome.
