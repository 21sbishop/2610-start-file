City Park

Orem City Park is the green heart of the city. Winding paths follow a small creek under tall shade trees, and wide lawns leave plenty of room for picnics, frisbee, and summer concerts.

It's an easy walk from downtown and a favorite spot for families, dog walkers, and anyone who needs a quiet afternoon outside.

Features

  • Walking paths and a creekside trail
  • Covered picnic pavilions you can reserve
  • Playground and splash pad
  • Off-leash dog area
  • Free summer concerts on the lawn
