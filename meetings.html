City Council Meetings

The Orem City Council meets on the first and third Thursday of each month at 6:00 p.m. in the City Council Chambers. The Council makes decisions on city policies, budgets, ordinances, and other matters that guide the future of our community. Each meeting includes a public forum, where residents are welcome to share their comments or concerns.
