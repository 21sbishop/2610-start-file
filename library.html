Library

The Orem Public Library is a bright, welcoming place to read, study, and gather. Its tall windows fill the reading rooms with daylight, and quiet corners on every floor make it easy to settle in with a good book.

Beyond books, the library offers free programs for every age, from toddler story time to job-search help and technology classes for adults.

Features

  • More than 200,000 books, audiobooks, and movies
  • Children's area with weekly story time
  • Free Wi-Fi, computers, and printing
  • Study rooms you can reserve online
  • Local history collection about Orem and Utah Valley
